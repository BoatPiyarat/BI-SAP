## Note (non-blocking)

Since a brand-new `check_name`'s first day compares against a zero baseline (by design, and tested),
a check with a genuine pre-existing backlog that's newly instrumented will alert immediately unless
Boat sets a generous first threshold or a human manually seeds day-zero history before turning
alerting on. Recommend one line in the eventual deploy runbook describing this onboarding sequence
for a new check_name, so it's a deliberate step rather than a surprise the first time it happens.
