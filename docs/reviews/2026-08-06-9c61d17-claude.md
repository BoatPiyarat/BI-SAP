The finding correctly separates "the daily workflow can run unattended inside the current OPEN
month" (true — nothing in the nightly path reads or depends on the cutoff calendar during normal
operation) from "it cannot advance the accounting period unattended" (also true, given the
zero-caller result above) — this is not an overstated claim; the gap is specifically at the month
boundary, not a claim that daily operation is broken generally.

## Checked — cutoff ownership, no-inference, fail-closed conclusion

The finding explicitly assigns cutoff *values* to Finance ("Finance remains owner of later monthly
cutoffs") while assigning the *mechanism* gap to engineering — it does not attempt to invent a
September cutoff or guess a calendar, and explicitly states "running against an expired OPEN period
should stop and alert" rather than silently continuing. This matches the project's standing
fail-closed philosophy rather than proposing a workaround.

`sp_register_period_cutoff` for exactly-one future approved cutoff with validation, and
