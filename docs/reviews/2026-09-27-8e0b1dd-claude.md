## Findings

**A1 — pipeline lag is not separated; the headline overstates missing (checklist 7/12).**
Splitting `paid_missing.csv` by `paid_date`:

| paid_date | links | THB (distinct charges) |
|---|---:|---:|
| ≤ 2026-09-24 | 496 | — |
| 2026-09-25 | 216 | 838,469.59 |
| 2026-09-26 | 629 | 1,984,746.00 |

The audit ran at 26 Sep 23:06 ICT. A charge paid on 26 Sep cannot be in SAP_LIVE_FULL before
this sequence completes: export (01:30 on the 27th), then SAP import, then mirror extract (20:30 on
the 27th). Among the 845 links paid on 25–26 Sep, 476 are `SAP_PERIOD_PENDING_RECEIPT_MISSING`.
So the "528 Pending, gap is after RCL 05_newpayment" row is **mostly in-flight work, not a defect**.
Add a `WITHIN_PIPELINE_LAG` bucket (paid after the last completed export → import → extract cycle)
to the operator query, so a mid-month run does not read as 1,310 missing. Re-run at least two
cycles later before quoting a backlog. The real candidate backlog is about 496 links
(paid ≤ 24 Sep), plus whatever the 25 Sep rows do not clear tonight.

**A2 — no-order-link (435) needs an age and product profile before it is called a linkage defect.**
Aggregates: 274 FULL_PAYMENT / 144 RABBIT_CARE_INSTALLMENT / 17 bank installment. By provider:
145 RABBIT_LENDING, 118 RCB, 100 SERVICE_PROVIDER_UNSPECIFIED (all DIRECT_PAYMENT), 36 OMISE and
others. 3,878,549.95 THB. 138 are from 25–26 Sep, so CareOS order creation lagging the charge is
possible. The 100 unspecified-provider direct payments may be a different flow (credit-shell
or transfer) rather than a missing link. Confirm with CareOS owners before it becomes a fix item.

**A3 — minor.** 10 `ADDITIONAL_CHARGE_PAID_PERIOD_GATE` links were measured after the 21:52
dashboard deploy. They confirm that the live `RCL 05_newpayment` still blocks extras, which is
consistent with the 08522b2 review.

**A4 — data handling.** About 11k transaction-level rows (order, charge and invoice IDs, amounts)
are committed to the GitHub repo. There are no personal fields, but this is business-confidential
data. Confirm that the repo's access matches that sensitivity, or keep row-level CSVs outside git
with only hashes and aggregates committed.
