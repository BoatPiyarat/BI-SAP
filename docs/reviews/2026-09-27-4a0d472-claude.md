Step 6 of the fix plan ("inspect payload→file→import log for 703 Paid") should shrink to the
**25** older links after one more export cycle. The structural groups are mostly older
payments. Those are real, age-independent defects, and the plan correctly prioritises them.

**S2 — EDC-KBANK mis-mapping is a live accounting defect; prioritise it.**
88 links / 87 items would carry `RCB-EDC-KBANK` although the source is non-EDC. This view feeds
the live RCB Motor export (step 03 of `rcb-motor-order-payment-sap-bucket-1`, which ran at 01:30
on 2026-09-27). SAP maps the payment channel to a bank or clearing account, so rows already
imported may be sitting in the wrong account. Add a check: SAP_LIVE_FULL rows with PaymentChannel
`RCB-EDC-KBANK` whose CareOS charge payment_method ≠ EDC, over all history, not just September.
That gives the size of the existing mis-posting separately from the forward fix.

**S3 — the order-year cutoff also constrains the additional-receipt fix.** The live wrapper's
`OrderDate >= 2026-01-01` filter (85 links) also drops extra receipts on 2025 orders. Add this
filter to the acceptance population of the RCL additional-receipts handoff
(`docs/HANDOFF_QUEUE.md`, 2026-09-26 21:00). Otherwise the conservation test passes while 2025
extras are silently excluded.

**S4 — duplicate owners (135 Paid + 24 cancel rows in more than one view).** Correctly flagged as
unproven duplicates. Before any fix, add one check against the archived files, or SAP DocEntry
multiplicity per (OrderItem, Period, InvoiceNo). That shows whether the overlap has already
produced double documents (the 423 multi-DocEntry REVIEW links in 8e0b1dd may be this).
